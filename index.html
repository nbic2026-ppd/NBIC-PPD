National Biz-Inno Challenge 2026

NBIC 2026 ialah pertandingan inovasi dan keusahawanan peringkat kebangsaan yang menyediakan platform kepada pasukan pelajar untuk mengetengahkan idea, inovasi dan penyelesaian bernilai melalui penilaian profesional serta pendedahan strategik. NBIC 2026 is a national-level innovation and entrepreneurship competition providing student teams with a platform to present ideas, innovations and value-driven solutions through professional evaluation and strategic exposure.

⏳ Pendaftaran & penghantaran abstrak ditutup pada 14 Ogos 2026, 11.59 malam (MYT). Registration & abstract submission close on 14 August 2026, 11.59 PM (MYT).

Panduan PenyertaanParticipation Information

Kelayakan dan Komposisi PasukanEligibility and Team Composition

Penyertaan terbuka kepada pelajar aktif daripada institusi yang layak. Tiada had umur minimum atau maksimum, namun semua peserta hendaklah berstatus pelajar aktif di institusi masing-masing.Participation is open to active students from eligible institutions. There is no minimum or maximum age requirement; however, all participants must be actively enrolled at their respective institutions.

01

Institusi LayakEligible Institutions

Politeknik Malaysia, Kolej Komuniti, Universiti Awam, IPTS, Kolej Universiti dan institusi TVET yang diiktiraf.Malaysian Polytechnics, Community Colleges, Public Universities, Private HEIs, University Colleges and recognised TVET institutions.

Lihat syarat penuhView full rules →
02

Komposisi PasukanTeam Composition

Setiap pasukan hendaklah terdiri daripada seorang (1) pensyarah/penyelia dan empat (4) orang pelajar. Jumlah: lima (5) orang.Each team shall consist of one (1) lecturer/supervisor and four (4) students. Total: five (5) members.

Semak komposisiCheck composition →
03

Keperluan PenyeliaSupervisor Requirement

Setiap pasukan mesti diselia oleh seorang pensyarah daripada institusi masing-masing yang bertindak sebagai pembimbing dan penghubung rasmi.Each team must be supervised by a lecturer from its institution who acts as mentor and official liaison.

Struktur PenilaianEvaluation Structure

Dua komponen. Satu markah akhir.Two components. One final score.

Setiap penyertaan dinilai melalui Poster dan Video Pitching dengan wajaran yang sama.Each entry is evaluated through Poster and Video Pitching with equal weighting.

Poster

50%

Kandungan, inovasi, reka bentuk visual dan potensi komersial.Content, innovation, visual design and commercial potential.

Video Pitching

50%

Kandungan, penyampaian, produksi dan daya tarikan. Tempoh maksimum: 5 minit.Content, delivery, production and audience appeal. Maximum duration: 5 minutes.

Soalan LazimFrequently Asked Questions

Maklumat penting sebelum mendaftarImportant information before registration

Siapakah yang layak menyertai NBIC 2026?Who is eligible to participate in NBIC 2026?
Pelajar aktif daripada Politeknik Malaysia, Kolej Komuniti, Universiti Awam, IPTS, Kolej Universiti dan institusi TVET yang diiktiraf, tertakluk kepada syarat rasmi.Active students from Malaysian Polytechnics, Community Colleges, Public Universities, Private HEIs, University Colleges and recognised TVET institutions, subject to the official rules.
Apakah komposisi setiap pasukan?What is the required team composition?
Satu (1) pensyarah/penyelia dan empat (4) orang pelajar; jumlah lima (5) orang setiap pasukan.One (1) lecturer/supervisor and four (4) students; five (5) members in total.
Apakah yuran penyertaan?What are the registration fees?
Kategori A (Politeknik & Kolej Komuniti): RM150. Kategori B (IPT terbuka): RM120 kadar early bird seperti yang dinyatakan dalam maklumat pendaftaran rasmi.Category A (Polytechnics & Community Colleges): RM150. Category B (open higher education institutions): RM120 early-bird rate as stated in the official registration information.
Bilakah pendaftaran ditutup?When does registration close?
Pendaftaran dan penghantaran abstrak ditutup pada 14 Ogos 2026, jam 11.59 malam (MYT).Registration and abstract submission close on 14 August 2026 at 11.59 PM (MYT).
