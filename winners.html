When will NBIC 2026 results be published?Bilakah keputusan NBIC 2026 akan diterbitkan?
The official results were announced at the exhibition on 15 October 2026 and are published on this page.Keputusan rasmi telah diumumkan semasa pameran pada 15 Oktober 2026 dan diterbitkan di halaman ini.
How will finalists be notified?Bagaimanakah finalis dimaklumkan?
The 20 finalists will receive an official call letter (surat panggilan) for the exhibition and the announcement of the main prize winners.20 finalis akan menerima surat panggilan rasmi bagi pameran dan pengumuman pemenang hadiah utama.
Where should participants check for official results?Di mana peserta perlu menyemak keputusan rasmi?
