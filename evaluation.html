Poster preparation guidelinesGaris panduan penyediaan poster

  • A1 size (594mm × 841mm), portrait orientation.Saiz A1 (594mm × 841mm), orientasi potret.
  • PDF format. Maximum file size is awaiting confirmation by the secretariat.Format PDF. Had maksimum saiz fail masih menunggu pengesahan urus setia.
  • Include project title, team name, institution, introduction, objectives, methodology, findings, impact and conclusion.Sertakan tajuk projek, nama pasukan, institusi, pengenalan, objektif, metodologi, hasil dapatan, impak dan kesimpulan.
  • Third-party copyrighted images, logos or materials may not be used without permission.Imej, logo atau bahan berhak cipta pihak ketiga tidak boleh digunakan tanpa kebenaran.
  • Submission platform and exact closing time are awaiting confirmation.Platform penghantaran dan masa tutup sebenar masih menunggu pengesahan.

Video Pitching preparation guidelinesGaris panduan penyediaan Video Pitching

  • Maximum duration: five minutes. Longer videos may lose marks, and judges may stop evaluation at minute five.Tempoh maksimum lima minit. Video lebih panjang boleh dipotong markah dan juri boleh menamatkan penilaian pada minit kelima.
  • MP4 format with a minimum resolution of 720p HD.Format MP4 dengan resolusi minimum 720p HD.
  • Presentation may be in Bahasa Melayu or English, delivered clearly and professionally.Penyampaian boleh menggunakan Bahasa Melayu atau Bahasa Inggeris secara jelas dan profesional.
  • Cover the problem, innovative solution/product, demonstration where relevant, market/commercial potential and a brief call to action.Rangkumi masalah, penyelesaian/produk inovasi, demonstrasi jika berkaitan, potensi pasaran/komersialisasi dan seruan tindakan ringkas.
  • Upload platform, submission form and exact closing time are awaiting confirmation.Platform muat naik, borang penghantaran dan masa tutup sebenar masih menunggu pengesahan.
  • Unlicensed third-party music, video clips or copyrighted materials are not permitted.Muzik, klip video atau bahan berhak cipta pihak ketiga tanpa lesen tidak dibenarkan.
General rulesPeraturan am

Integrity and eligibility.Integriti dan kelayakan.

  1. Entries must be the team's original work and must not have previously won a national or international innovation/entrepreneurship competition.Penyertaan mestilah hasil kerja asli pasukan dan tidak pernah memenangi pertandingan inovasi/keusahawanan kebangsaan atau antarabangsa sebelum ini.
  2. Plagiarism, idea copying or duplicate entries with another team will result in immediate disqualification.Plagiarisme, ciplak idea atau pertindihan penyertaan dengan pasukan lain menyebabkan diskualifikasi serta-merta.
  3. Late or non-compliant submissions will not be evaluated, except for emergencies verified by the secretariat.Penghantaran lewat atau tidak mematuhi format tidak akan dinilai, kecuali kecemasan yang disahkan urus setia.
  4. The secretariat may request clarification or supporting evidence such as prototype proof or research data.Urus setia boleh meminta klarifikasi atau bukti sokongan seperti bukti prototaip atau data kajian.
  5. Judges are appointed by NBIC 2026 and comprise industry, academic and entrepreneurship experts. Their decision is final.Panel Juri dilantik oleh NBIC 2026 dan terdiri daripada pakar industri, akademik dan keusahawanan. Keputusan mereka adalah muktamad.
  6. The secretariat may amend these guidelines. Changes will be announced through official competition channels.Urus setia boleh meminda garis panduan ini. Pindaan akan diumumkan melalui saluran rasmi pertandingan.
