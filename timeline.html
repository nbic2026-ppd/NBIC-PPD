Official event flow.Garis masa rasmi secara indikatif.

The competition journey typically moves from launch and registration through submission, review and final recognition.Perjalanan pertandingan biasanya bergerak daripada pelancaran dan pendaftaran hingga penghantaran, penilaian dan pengiktirafan akhir.

01 – 14 August 2026

Registration & Abstract SubmissionPendaftaran & Penghantaran Abstrak

Complete registration and submit the competition abstract by 14 August 2026.Lengkapkan pendaftaran dan hantar abstrak pertandingan selewat-lewatnya 14 Ogos 2026.

15 – 28 August 2026

Notice of Acceptance & PaymentNotis Penerimaan & Tempoh Pembayaran

Acceptance notices are issued and accepted participants complete the payment process.Notis penerimaan dikeluarkan dan peserta yang diterima melengkapkan proses pembayaran.

29 August – 6 September 2026

Poster & Video SubmissionPenghantaran Poster & Video

Submit the required poster and video materials by 6 September 2026.Hantar bahan poster dan video yang diperlukan selewat-lewatnya 6 September 2026.

7 – 25 September 2026

Preliminary Round JudgingPenilaian Peringkat Saringan

Submitted entries proceed through the preliminary judging period.Penyertaan yang dihantar melalui tempoh penilaian peringkat saringan.

13 – 14 October 2026

Final Judging & Closing CeremonyPenilaian Akhir & Majlis Penutup

The competition concludes with final judging and the closing ceremony.Pertandingan berakhir dengan penilaian akhir dan majlis penutup.
