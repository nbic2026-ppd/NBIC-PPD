Official rulesPeraturan rasmi

NBIC 2026 Rules & Regulations.Syarat & Peraturan NBIC 2026.

The official competition rules confirmed by the NBIC 2026 secretariat, covering eligibility through to prizes and intellectual property.Syarat pertandingan rasmi yang disahkan oleh urus setia NBIC 2026, merangkumi kelayakan sehingga hadiah dan harta intelek.

1. Eligibility1. Kelayakan Penyertaan

Participation is open to students from the following institutions:Penyertaan adalah terbuka kepada pelajar daripada institusi berikut:

  • All Malaysian PolytechnicsSemua Politeknik Malaysia
  • Malaysian Community CollegesKolej Komuniti Malaysia
  • Public Universities (UA)Universiti Awam (UA)
  • Private Higher Education Institutions (IPTS)Institusi Pengajian Tinggi Swasta (IPTS)
  • University CollegesKolej Universiti
  • Recognised TVET InstitutionsInstitusi TVET yang diiktiraf

2. Age Requirement2. Kelayakan Had Umur

There is no minimum or maximum age requirement. However, all participants must be actively enrolled as students at their respective institutions.Tiada had umur minimum atau maksimum ditetapkan. Walau bagaimanapun, semua peserta mestilah berstatus pelajar aktif di institusi masing-masing.

3. Team Composition3. Bilangan Ahli Pasukan

Each team shall consist of:Setiap pasukan hendaklah terdiri daripada:

  • One (1) Lecturer/SupervisorSeorang (1) Pensyarah/Penyelia
  • Four (4) StudentsEmpat (4) orang Pelajar
  • Total: Five (5) members per team.Jumlah keseluruhan: Lima (5) orang bagi setiap pasukan.

4. Adviser Requirement4. Keperluan Penyelia

Each participating team must be supervised by one (1) lecturer from their respective institution. The supervisor's responsibilities include:Setiap penyertaan hendaklah mempunyai seorang pensyarah penyelia daripada institusi masing-masing. Peranan penyelia adalah seperti berikut:

  • Providing guidance and mentorship throughout the project.Memberikan bimbingan dan panduan sepanjang pelaksanaan projek.
  • Ensuring that the submitted project is original.Memastikan projek yang dikemukakan adalah asli.
  • Serving as the official liaison between the organising committee and the team.Bertindak sebagai pegawai penghubung rasmi antara pasukan dan pihak penganjur.

Supervisors are not permitted to assist their teams during the pitching session.Penyelia tidak dibenarkan membantu pasukan semasa sesi pitching berlangsung.

5. Number of Entries5. Bilangan Penyertaan

  • There is no limit to the number of entries submitted by each institution.Tiada had bilangan penyertaan daripada setiap institusi.
  • Each student may participate in only one (1) team.Setiap pelajar hanya dibenarkan menyertai satu (1) pasukan sahaja.
  • A lecturer may supervise more than one (1) team.Seorang pensyarah dibenarkan menyelia lebih daripada satu (1) pasukan.

6. Previous Project Eligibility6. Kelayakan Projek Terdahulu

Projects that have previously participated in other competitions are eligible for NBIC, provided that:Projek yang pernah menyertai pertandingan lain layak menyertai NBIC dengan syarat:

  • The project remains the original work and intellectual property of the participants.Projek tersebut masih merupakan hak milik peserta.
  • The project has not previously won any national or international innovation or entrepreneurship competition.Projek tersebut tidak pernah memenangi mana-mana pertandingan inovasi atau keusahawanan peringkat kebangsaan atau antarabangsa.
  • The project has been enhanced with improvements and/or additional commercialisation value.Projek tersebut telah melalui penambahbaikan serta mempunyai nilai tambah dari aspek inovasi dan/atau pengkomersialan.

Stage 3 – Final CompetitionPeringkat 3 – Pertandingan Akhir

  • Live PitchingPembentangan (Live Pitching)
  • Question & Answer (Q&A) SessionSesi Soal Jawab (Q&A)

Main AwardsHadiah Utama

  • Gold – Plaque, Certificate and Cash PrizeEmas – Plak, Sijil dan Wang Tunai
  • Silver – Plaque, Certificate and Cash PrizePerak – Plak, Sijil dan Wang Tunai
  • Bronze – Plaque, Certificate and Cash PrizeGangsa – Plak, Sijil dan Wang Tunai

Special AwardsAnugerah Khas

9. Intellectual Property9. Harta Intelek

The intellectual property rights of all submitted projects remain the property of the participants. NBIC reserves the right to use photographs and videos of the projects solely for promotional purposes and official reporting.Hak harta intelek bagi semua projek yang dipertandingkan kekal menjadi milik peserta. NBIC berhak menggunakan gambar dan video projek bagi tujuan promosi serta laporan rasmi pertandingan.

10. Language10. Bahasa Pengantaraan

Presentations may be delivered in either:Pembentangan boleh disampaikan dalam:

  • Bahasa MelayuBahasa Melayu
  • EnglishBahasa Inggeris

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.Soalan lazim.

Is NBIC 2026 fully online?Adakah NBIC 2026 sepenuhnya dalam talian?

Yes. The supplied information states that the competition is conducted fully online and hosted by Politeknik Premier Port Dickson.Ya. Maklumat yang dibekalkan menyatakan bahawa pertandingan dilaksanakan sepenuhnya dalam talian dan dianjurkan oleh Politeknik Premier Port Dickson.

What must be submitted first?Apakah yang perlu dihantar dahulu?

Registration and the abstract must be completed by 14 August 2026.Pendaftaran dan abstrak mesti dilengkapkan selewat-lewatnya 14 Ogos 2026.

When are poster and video materials due?Bilakah poster dan video perlu dihantar?

The deadline is 6 September 2026.Tarikh akhir ialah 6 September 2026.

What are the registration fees?Berapakah yuran pendaftaran?

Category A (PolyCC) is RM150. Category B (Open Higher Education Institutions) lists an early-bird fee of RM120.Kategori A (PolyCC) ialah RM150. Kategori B (Institusi Pengajian Tinggi Terbuka) menyenaraikan yuran early bird RM120.

What are the judging criteria?Apakah kriteria penilaian?

Poster contributes 50% and the five-minute Video Pitching contributes 50%. View the Innovation Evaluation page for the complete criteria and guidelines.Poster menyumbang 50% dan Video Pitching lima minit menyumbang 50%. Rujuk halaman Penilaian Inovasi untuk kriteria dan garis panduan lengkap. View evaluation guidelines →Lihat garis panduan penilaian →

What awards are offered?Apakah anugerah yang ditawarkan?

Gold, Silver and Bronze main awards (plaque, certificate and cash prize), plus Best Prototype and Best Presentation special awards. See the Rules & Regulations above for the full list. Exact cash amounts are not yet stated.Anugerah utama Emas, Perak dan Gangsa (plak, sijil dan wang tunai), serta anugerah khas Prototaip Terbaik dan Pembentangan Terbaik. Rujuk Syarat & Peraturan di atas untuk senarai penuh. Jumlah wang tunai sebenar belum dinyatakan.

Confirmed details are enough to start preparing.Butiran yang disahkan sudah cukup untuk mula bersedia.

Still have a question that isn't covered above? The secretariat is ready to help before you submit.Masih ada soalan yang tidak dijawab di atas? Urus setia sedia membantu sebelum anda menghantar.
