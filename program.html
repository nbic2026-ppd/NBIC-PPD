Ringkasan Program Programme Overview

National Biz-Inno Challenge 2026 National Biz-Inno Challenge 2026

NBIC 2026 ialah pertandingan inovasi dan keusahawanan peringkat kebangsaan yang menyediakan platform kepada pasukan pelajar untuk mengetengahkan idea, inovasi dan penyelesaian bernilai melalui penilaian profesional serta pendedahan strategik. NBIC 2026 is a national-level innovation and entrepreneurship competition that provides student teams with a platform to present ideas, innovations and value-driven solutions through professional evaluation and strategic exposure.

Mengenai NBIC 2026 About NBIC 2026

Memacu inovasi. Menginspirasikan usahawan masa hadapan. Driving innovation. Inspiring future entrepreneurs.

Jabatan Pendidikan Politeknik dan Kolej Komuniti (JPPKK), Kementerian Pendidikan Tinggi bersama Politeknik Premier Port Dickson menganjurkan edisi sulung NBIC 2026 sebagai platform inovasi dan keusahawanan peringkat kebangsaan. The Department of Polytechnic and Community College Education (JPPKK), Ministry of Higher Education, together with Politeknik Premier Port Dickson, organises the inaugural NBIC 2026 as a national platform for innovation and entrepreneurship.

01

Pendedahan KebangsaanNational Exposure

Pasukan berpeluang mengetengahkan hasil inovasi pada platform peringkat kebangsaan dan membina kredibiliti projek.Teams gain an opportunity to showcase their innovation on a national platform and strengthen project credibility.

02

Penilaian ProfesionalProfessional Evaluation

Penyertaan dinilai melalui komponen Poster dan Video Pitching dengan wajaran sama bagi menentukan keputusan akhir.Entries are evaluated through equally weighted Poster and Video Pitching components to determine the final result.

03

Nilai & Potensi PasaranValue & Market Potential

Pertandingan memberi ruang kepada pasukan untuk memperkemas nilai, kerelevanan dan potensi komersial idea atau inovasi.The competition provides teams with a platform to strengthen the value, relevance and commercial potential of an idea or innovation.

04

Jaringan StrategikStrategic Networking

NBIC 2026 membuka ruang hubungan antara peserta, institusi, industri dan bakal kolaborator.NBIC 2026 creates opportunities for connections among participants, institutions, industry and potential collaborators.

Kelayakan Penyertaan Participation Eligibility

Terbuka kepada pelajar aktif institusi yang layak. Open to active students from eligible institutions.

Tiada had umur minimum atau maksimum ditetapkan. Walau bagaimanapun, semua peserta mestilah berstatus pelajar aktif di institusi masing-masing. There is no minimum or maximum age requirement. However, all participants must be actively enrolled as students at their respective institutions.

Komposisi PasukanTeam Composition

Satu pasukan = 1 Pensyarah/Penyelia + 4 PelajarOne team = 1 Lecturer/Supervisor + 4 Students

Jumlah rasmi setiap pasukan ialah lima (5) orang. Setiap pasukan mesti diselia oleh seorang pensyarah daripada institusi masing-masing.The official team size is five (5) members. Each team must be supervised by a lecturer from its respective institution.
